In the last 3–5 years, the rapid development of artificial intelligence has unveiled radically new possibilities across many industries, including urban planning and architecture. With the recent emergence of large generative models like MidJourney and ChatGPT, architects and spatial designers have enthusiastically adopted them in their daily work. However, these AI tools are currently primarily used for creating visualizations (still far from perfect, by the way) and drafting explanatory notes.
